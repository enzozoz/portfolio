about me

Hi, I'm Enzo.

I'm a Computer Science student at UT Austin, graduating in Spring 2028 with a minor in Business. I enjoy identifying inefficiencies, solving problems, and building software that makes processes more effective and accessible.

Oh! I also really like to wear hats.

Currently, I work as a Support Advisor at Apple and serve as one of the Creative Directors for the UT Filipino Student Association, one of the largest Asian organizations on campus. These roles have strengthened my ability to communicate, collaborate, and create solutions in both technical and creative environments.

Outside of technology, I'm a dancer and choreographer. Through dance, I've developed discipline, creativity, and teamwork — qualities that continue to shape the way I approach software development and leadership.

I'm currently seeking Summer 2027 internship opportunities and am always excited to connect with others who are passionate about technology, innovation, and building meaningful solutions!
